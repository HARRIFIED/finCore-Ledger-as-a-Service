## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL-3.0 is a strong copyleft license. You need a commercial license if **any** of the
following apply and you do not want to comply with the AGPL:

- You want to **embed finCore in a closed-source product** or distribute it without releasing your
  own source under the AGPL.
- You **operate a modified finCore as a hosted/SaaS offering** and do not want to make your
  modified source available to your users (AGPL §13 requires this).
- Your organization's policy prohibits AGPL-licensed code, or you need warranties, indemnification,
  or a support SLA that the AGPL's "as is" terms do not provide.

You do **not** need a commercial license to:

- Evaluate, run, or self-host **unmodified** finCore internally.
- Modify finCore for internal use, **provided** you do not distribute it or offer it as a network
  service to third parties — or, if you do, you comply fully with the AGPL (including source
  disclosure under §13).

## What the commercial license grants

The exact grant is defined in the signed agreement, but commercial licenses typically provide:

- A license to use, modify, and distribute finCore **without** the AGPL's copyleft/source-disclosure
  obligations.
- The right to embed finCore in proprietary products and hosted services.
- Optional support, maintenance, and update entitlements.
- Warranty and indemnification terms as negotiated.
